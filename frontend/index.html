Why it works Built for the decision, not the deck.

The owner types a business name. Nothing else. Scout adapters read every public trace they can reach, and the swarm's personas and segments emerge from that evidence — nothing about a café, a clinic, or a startup is hardcoded. The pipeline is generic; the content is yours.

The debate is grounded

  • —Evidence cited from real sources — every persona argues from what the internet actually says
  • —Camps read each other's strongest points before their second turn
  • —Fabricated evidence is demoted to neutral, so bluffing can't win a vote
  • —Borderline tallies become low-confidence verdicts — that's honesty, a feature

And the swarm keeps getting better

Every proposal is tested against a calibrated mock market — seeded truth hidden from the agents, beliefs rewritten by outcomes, keep-if-better. When the loop stops improving a plan, the verdict says so.

  • —Calibration proposes ranges from reviews; truth is sampled inside them
  • —Train/test splits with shifted contexts — no memorizing one market
  • —The naive plan ("open till 2 AM + promo everywhere") fails on most seeds
  • —In production: owners log real outcomes, and the swarm learns from those
